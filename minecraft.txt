THE PROBLEM (confirmed by a diagnostic): the deployed model uses argmax, but it was
TRAINED with stochastic sampling (PPO + entropy bonus) and its best checkpoint was
SELECTED by sampled reward. Under pure argmax (no safety overrides) the greedy policy
collapses to a sticky, near-arbitrary single phase per junction. A diagnostic on
type1_low (argmax + governor, NO overrides) produced this per-junction phase distribution
(% of decisions on each phase):

  junction   NS-thru  N-left  S-left  EW-thru  E-left  W-left
  J0            0%      2%      6%      2%      90%     0%
  J1            3%      0%      0%      0%      95%     2%
  J2            1%      0%      0%      2%      97%     0%
  J3           46%     20%      9%     22%      1%      2%
  J4            0%      0%      4%     13%      0%     83%

4 of 5 junctions lock onto ONE phase 83-97% of the time and starve the rest -> ~12-21
cars sit forever -> the gridlock detector trips at low/medium traffic. (It does NOT
gridlock at high traffic, because heavy queues create strong reward gradients that keep
the policy moving the bulk.) Today this is masked in production by hand-coded "starvation
overrides" (force a through phase after 8 idle decisions, force the starved through
direction after 10, force a starved left after 15). Those overrides are a band-aid; we
want the POLICY itself to stop starving so the overrides become a rarely-triggered safety
net rather than load-bearing.

ROOT CAUSES (the problems you are fixing):
  P1 Train/deploy mismatch: trained + checkpoint-selected under SAMPLING, deployed under
     ARGMAX. The greedy mode was never optimized or even measured.
  P2 Flat reward at low demand: pressure/queue/throughput terms ~0 with 0-3 cars/lane, so
     logits barely discriminate and argmax is arbitrary & sticky.
  P3 No anti-starvation signal in the reward: RewardWeights.fairness = 0.0, and the
     `starvation` term only fires when phase_duration > 30s AND demand exists. Nothing
     teaches "serve every movement."
  P4 The governor cements the lock instead of breaking it: anti-flicker penalizes
     returning to a phase, min-green forces holding, and pressure-boost only fires above
     pressure_thresh = 0.35 (never met at balanced low demand).
  P5 (minor) Low-signal observations: counts are normalized by /15 and /30, so low-traffic
     features are all ~0.0-0.2 and nearly identical across phases.

GOAL: keep ARGMAX as the deployment/eval inference mode, but make the greedy policy good
enough that, with the governor and WITHOUT the starvation overrides, no junction starves a
movement that has demand, and there is no gridlock at any traffic level — while NOT
regressing high-traffic performance.
